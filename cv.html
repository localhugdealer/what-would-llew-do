Profile

IT Solutions Engineer with more than 10 years of experience bridging enterprise customers and technical delivery. I spent five years at VMware Carbon Black as a Specialist Solutions Engineer across EMEA, running architecture advisory, demos and proof of concepts for enterprise security deployments.

From 2023 to 2026 I founded and directed Nova Mentis, where I built Nova Nexus, a production multi-tenant AI decision support platform in Python with Pydantic AI and the OpenAI API, and opened it to 40+ early access users. I now make games as LocalHugDealer Studios.

What ties it together is a habit of asking "why" until the real problem shows up, then explaining the answer in plain English. I'm comfortable moving between a customer architecture conversation and the codebase in the same day, and I get real satisfaction from coaching the people around me.

How I got here

The short version of how a kid at the family computer ended up in cybersecurity, AI and game design.

  1. 2004 to 2015

    School years

    King Edward VI Preparatory, St John's Preparatory and St John's College. IT, Advanced Mathematics and Dramatic Arts in the classroom, rowing, hockey and cricket outside it.

  2. 2016

    A gap year and a fork in the road

    During a gap year I studied CompTIA Security+ through New Mexico Ruidoso College. I was booked on a game design course at Learn3D for 2017, then a job offer came along and I took the career safe route into IT. Game design stayed with me in every spare moment after that.

  3. 2016

    Into cybersecurity

    Joined Netxactics, a security distributor, supporting partners and customers on Sophos, Rapid7, Thycotic, Flexera and Altaro, and running my first pre-sales demos.

  4. 2017

    Managed security

    Took on work for Obsequium, Netxactics' sister company, looking after managed customer environments: firewalls, Rapid7 InsightIDR and Nexpose, and Sophos deployments.

  5. 2019

    A move to the UK

    Moved to the UK in April and joined Carbon Black as a Solutions Engineer a few weeks later. Carbon Black became part of VMware later that year.

  6. 2020

    Lockdown and the Digital Consultant

    Started the VMware Digital Consultant project, which grew to 70+ multilingual security assessments. Lockdown was a difficult stretch, spent largely alone in a small flat, and it planted the seed for Nova Nexus: the personalised guidance I needed and couldn't find.

  7. 2021

    MVP of the year

    Named MVP of FY21 among all solution engineers globally across the business unit, and received the Perseverance Award from the Dell Synergy Team for getting the Dell channel up and running.

  8. 2022

    Analytics and philosophy

    Built the Solution Engineering Analytics Suite in Power BI, adopted as standard tooling across the group. Began studying Advaita Vedanta at the School of Practical Philosophy.

  9. 2023

    Mentoring and a new company

    Designed and led a mentorship programme for commercial solution engineers that lifted product knowledge by 20% to 40%. Completed VMware's Aspiring Manager programme, and founded Nova Mentis in August.

  10. 2024

    Moving on from VMware

    After Broadcom acquired VMware and restructured the team, I chose to move on in February rather than continue through a contractor route. Started building Nova Nexus in November.

  11. Late 2024 to 2026

    Skill Forge

    Through Nova Mentis and as an official ScoreApp partner, built lead generation scorecards for coaches, consultancies, and legal and data firms, and ran masterclasses, scorecard audits and marketing direction workshops.

  12. Early 2026

    Nova Nexus early access

    Opened early access to 40+ users, with one-on-one vision workshops and demo sessions. Selected for the City of Cape Town Youth Start Entrepreneurial Challenge, one of 150 participants from 700+ applicants.

  13. Mid 2026

    Game design, finally

    Put Nova Mentis on hold and opened LocalHugDealer Studios. Learnt Godot in a week, then built Trust No 1 for the Brackeys Game Jam and placed #41 for enjoyment out of 2,248 ranked entries. Now building Enemy No. 1.

Grounded Citation Agent

Personal project

LangGraph agent that answers questions from a knowledge base and verifies its own citations. A verification node checks every cited source against what retrieval actually returned, catching hallucinated sources and uncited claims, and routes ungrounded answers back to the model for correction. Built to explore reliability patterns for production RAG systems.

Nova Nexus

Nov 2024 to Jul 2026, Nova Mentis

Multi-tenant AI vision and decision support platform, built and shipped solo in Python and Anvil. Pydantic AI orchestrates OpenAI structured-output calls against validated schemas. Includes per-call telemetry with tenant attribution, a prompt and schema registry, token-based billing with pre-deduction and per-tenant API key scoping. Semantic recommendations use text-embedding-3-small with cosine similarity blended with evaluation scores. Launched an early access programme with 40+ users.
