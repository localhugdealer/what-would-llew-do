How it works

Simple and low pressure. Here's how it goes.

  1. Send me a WhatsApp

    Tell me what you're stuck on in your own words. No jargon needed.

  2. We make a plan

    I ask "why" until the real problem shows up, then we work out how many sessions it's likely to take. Sometimes one chat is enough to sort it out.

  3. We work through it together

    I explain as we go, so you walk away knowing how to do it yourself, not just with something that works.

Hi, I'm Llewellyn. Llew for short.

Tech is my language, and translating it into plain English is the part I enjoy most.

Over the years friends, family and friends of friends have come to me with all sorts: printers that won't print, backups, Wi-Fi, Excel, and more recently how to use AI or how to start building a game. What Would Llew Do brings that together in one place, so anyone who is stuck has someone to ask.
